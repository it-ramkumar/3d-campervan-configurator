VAN HERO - how to put it on your website

1. Upload the whole "van-hero" folder to your website (index.html and van.glb must stay together).
2. Put this at the top of your home page, where the hero section goes:

   <iframe src="/van-hero/" style="width:100%;height:100vh;border:0;display:block" title="Van conversion"></iframe>

CHANGE THE TEXT AND BUTTONS
Open index.html in a text editor.
- Headline and paragraph: inside <section class="copy">.
- Buttons: change href="#contact" and href="#work" to your own page links.
- Build order and part names: find "EDIT HERE".

Note: open it from your website (a web server), not by double-clicking the file.
